# QA pass, Tuesday 8 September 2026

The device pass from the cue sheet's checklist: one person, one phone,
one laptop, about seventy-five minutes, walking every screen the four
showcase jobs touch and everything that changed this week. The aim is a
list of things that look or feel wrong on real devices, not a test of
whether the product works: CI, pgTAP and the Playwright journeys cover
that. Anything found is fixed the same day, then the tenant is reseeded
before rehearsal one.

## Before you start (10 min)

1. **Reseed first.** The seed is from Sunday evening, so the full Monday
   06:00 CrossFit is already in the past. Ask me to reseed, or run
   Actions, "Demo gym", `mode=teardown` then `mode=seed`, slug
   `demo-crossfit-good-life`, name `CrossFit Good Life`. After a morning
   reseed the full class becomes Tuesday 17:30, the first CrossFit after
   it.
2. **Four sessions**, as the cue sheet says: iPhone Safari normal tab as
   owner, Private tab as member01; laptop Chrome as owner, incognito as
   coach1. Reject the cookie banner in each. Logins are in the cue sheet.
3. **Fresh tabs.** Open each as a new tab so the tab title and icon
   shipped yesterday are what you see, not a cached one.
4. Keep a note open. One line per finding: device, login, URL, what you
   expected, what you saw. Screenshot anything visual. Send the whole
   list here in one go at the end rather than one at a time.

## What to look for on every screen

- The bottom dock floats and the page scrolls under it; it shrinks as
  you scroll down and grows back at the top, and a short drag on it
  toggles the size.
- The top row: Today or Back on the left, the date centred, the page's
  one action on the right. Nothing under the status bar, no white strip
  above the app, no solid block at the foot of the page.
- Nothing wraps mid-word, clips, or scrolls sideways. Tap targets are
  reachable with a thumb.
- Dark mode: toggle it from the account menu once per pass and repeat
  the screens you are on. Both schemes matter.
- Deep pages have a Back that goes where you came from.
- The browser tab reads "Section · Temple" and shows the magenta star.

## Pass A. Owner on the iPhone (20 min)

Follow Job 1 of the cue sheet, then widen out.

1. `/timeline`: the day's feed with three questions waiting. Scroll the
   feed; watch the dock. Page back a day with the arrow; the composer
   gives way to "Past days are the record"; tap Today.
2. Composer: type "show me Alice Adams" and send. The card, the reply,
   the keyboard behaviour when the composer is focused, and that nothing
   jumps when the keyboard closes.
3. The gear: the gym's doors in the popover. Open Members, a member's
   profile, and Back. Open Team, Plans, Communications, AI Front Desk,
   Billing, Settings tab. Each one: heading, cards, Back.
4. The avatar: the account menu. Switch to member view, then back to
   staff view. Messages. Account.
5. `/classes`: the date row, week strip, list. Open a class. Day, Week,
   Month. Add class from the row and cancel out of it.
6. `/programming`: a programmed day, the week strip dots, Sunday empty.
7. `/analysis` and `/setup` if they are reachable from the dock or the
   gear: a glance at layout only.
8. Rotate the phone once on Timeline and once on Classes: nothing should
   break, even if landscape is not the demo.

## Pass B. Member on the iPhone (15 min)

1. `/book`: the week strip with dots, today's list. Book tomorrow's

## Pass C. Laptop, owner then coach1 (20 min)

Follow Jobs 3 and 4 at laptop width, and check the width transitions.

1. Owner, `/timeline` in Chrome at full width: the rail on the left, the
   top bar, the account menu from the top right.
2. Drag the window narrower through about 1024 and 768 pixels: the rail
   gives way to section pills, then the dock and the phone layout. Drag
   it back. Nothing should overlap during the transitions.
3. Coach1 (incognito): Timeline lists her classes. Open tomorrow's 06:00
   CrossFit from `/classes`, Request cover, Yes ask them.
4. Owner: the cover question on Timeline, the amber "Still needs a
   coach" line on that day, claim it from the card, the Inbox Cover tab.
5. Owner, `/classes`: Add class with real values, it appears on the
   grid. Day, Week, Month, Bulk.
6. Owner, `/programming`, then a class's Message class: "Bring a skipping
   rope tomorrow." Member tab on the phone, `/inbox`: it has arrived.
7. Owner, `/management`, AI Front Desk: the pipeline and one
   conversation. Then Manage's search: type "errors" and open
   Diagnostics; it should say nothing has broken.
8. Composer at laptop width: "add a lead called Jo Ellis".

Each of these shipped since the last time you used the app. Look at

`app.jointemple.io/join/demo-ironworks` as coach1 and joining, which
adds her to the throwaway gym until the next reseed. Do it last, or skip

## Do not do during QA

Run the sim or an e2e reseed against the tenant, schedule a campaign,
touch Stripe, invite by email, or use the override and comp-grant
switches. None of these are in the showcase.
