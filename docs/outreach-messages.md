# Outreach messages — cold email + social

Ready-to-send copy for the first outreach push. Grounded in what's
actually live (see `docs/feature-inventory.md`) and the positioning the
marketing site already commits to (`jointemple.io`). Pricing referenced
throughout: **£200/month, all-inclusive** — no per-member fees, website
builder included, and Temple takes **no cut** of member payments (gyms
charge on their own connected Stripe).

Target: independent CrossFit / Hyrox / functional-fitness box owners
(UK-first). Everything below is written for that reader.

**Before you send:** for cold email under UK PECR, keep it B2B (owner's
business address), identify yourself and the company, and include a
one-line opt-out. Send from `nick@jointemple.io`, not a noreply address,
so replies land somewhere you read.

---

## The four differentiators to lead with

Pick one per message — don't stack them.

1. **Flat £200/mo, all-in.** Competitors bill per active member, so your
   software cost climbs every time you grow. Temple doesn't.
2. **You keep 100% of member payments.** Members are charged on your own
   Stripe; Temple takes no application fee. Many platforms clip 1-2%+ on
   top of Stripe.
3. **Switching is a CSV, not a project.** One-click import from Mindbody,
   PushPress, Glofox, Wodify or a spreadsheet — members, *live Stripe
   subscriptions adopted with no re-charge*, and full workout history.
4. **Built for CrossFit and Hyrox, not adapted for it.** Native WOD +
   Hyrox race tracking, PRs, leaderboards, injury tracker, member app on
   iOS/Android — under your gym's brand.

## Cold email

### Subject lines (test a few)

- `Quick question about [Gym Name]'s software`
- `Cut your gym software to £200 flat`
- `Switching off [Mindbody/Glofox] without losing your data`
- `Built Temple for boxes like [Gym Name]`
- `Your members' data, moved in an afternoon`

Keep it lowercase-ish and specific. Avoid "revolutionary", avoid emoji in
the subject — both trip spam filters and read as mass-mail.

### Personalisation tokens

Fill before sending — a generic blast underperforms:

- `[Gym Name]`, `[First name]`

## What NOT to claim

Keep the copy honest — it's a small market and owners talk:

- Don't promise white-labelled App Store apps. The member app is
  Temple-branded natively; per-gym branding is the web/PWA install and
  in-app theming. (See follow-ups doc, item 6.)
- Don't imply Temple processes payments for them — *they* connect Stripe;
  Temple facilitates, takes no fee, and never holds funds.
- "No cut of revenue" is true for member payments. Say £200/mo flat is the
  software cost, so there's no confusion.
